# Track


rack turns your iPhone into a GPS lap timer for track days. Draw the finish line once on a satellite map, then just drive: every lap is timed on its own, with a live delta to your best lap and a split for every sector.

AUTOMATIC TIMING
• The clock starts and stops when you cross the line. No buttons.
• Crossings are interpolated between GPS readings, not rounded to the nearest one.
• The delta compares you with your reference lap at the same point on track, not at the same second.
• Add sector lines to see where you gain or lose time.
• Circuits and point-to-point runs such as hill climbs.
• Invalidate a lap after an off, a yellow flag or traffic.

A HUD YOU CAN READ AT SPEED
One big lap time with the delta right below it: green when you're faster, red when you're slower. A g-force bubble that works however the phone is mounted, portrait and landscape layouts, and haptics for every lap and every personal best.

YOUR TRACKS
Search for a circuit and place the finish line on satellite imagery, or stand on the line and set it with the compass. Track recognizes the circuit when you arrive. Share a layout by link or QR code; your times stay yours.

VIDEO WITH TELEMETRY
Record your laps with lap time, delta, speed, g-forces and a live track map drawn on the video. You can also save the overlay on its own, with a transparent background, to put over footage from another camera.

ANALYSIS
Every lap keeps its full trace. Scrub speed and g-force charts, see where you brake and accelerate on the layout, and chase a ghost of your best lap. Records are kept per car and per circuit.

GARAGE
A card for each car, with setup notes such as tire pressures, brake pads, weight and power.

APPLE WATCH AND THE HEALTH APP
See your lap time and delta on your wrist, feel a tap for every lap, and follow your heart rate during the session. With your permission, sessions are saved to the Health app as workouts with heart rate and calories burned.

CARPLAY, LIVE ACTIVITIES AND WIDGETS
Open Track in CarPlay to see your tracks on a map, nearest first, and start a session from the car's screen. During the session it shows lap, sector, delta, splits, last and best. The running clock also stays in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen, and on the CarPlay Dashboard with iOS 26.

SIRI AND SHORTCUTS
Say "Start a track session in Track" and the timer is armed at the circuit you're at, with your car. Or ask "What was my last lap in Track".

FREE ROAM
Record any drive without a finish line and see the route painted by braking and acceleration.

WEATHER
Temperature, humidity, wind and recent rain are saved with every session, together with the track condition.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no analytics. Your laps, telemetry and videos stay on your iPhone and in your private iCloud.

FREE
Timing, video and analysis are free for everyone. An optional one-time purchase supports development and unlocks alternate app icons, an accent color and your signature on videos.

Track is designed for closed circuits and events where driving at speed is permitted. Follow the rules of the venue, mount your phone securely and never interact with it while driving.

Requires iOS 17 or later. Live Activities and widgets require iOS 18 or later. The Apple Watch app requires watchOS 10 or later.
